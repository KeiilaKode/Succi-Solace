My game description is incomplete and will be added to and updated continually. I just realized that I never paid attention to the ReadMe portion of my repo,
and only recently decided to log and detail the overall ideal and concept of the game. So more is to come for Succi Solace.

This Repo is an additional home, other than my laptop, for the game I've been building since mid August of this year-2026, called Succi Solace. I'm the sole 
developer, designer, "artist"/creator of all the mats, sprites, backgrounds, platforms, enemies, cutscenes, all UI layout and HUD features, and obviously
all the sprite sheets that make up the game. The only thing in the game that waas'nt made by me is the music for each level. The level designs paired with 
their specific themes and color pallets, help me determine what classical piano symphony/song I want or feel is best suited for each terrain. All music is 
in the Public Domain, from composers like, Chopin, Bach, and Beethoven, with a strong preference to Chopin over all. The only exception to that rule, so far,
is the Level One sound track which was written and composed solely by a hyper-creative and unique artist, and dear friend of mine, who goes by Digital-Down.
He can be found on GitHub at, https://github.com/Digital-Down, and while the absolute approval and blessing to use this song in the official and final version 
of my game, has yet to be granted to me, it remains to be, in my estimation, the only suitable level one sound track, that I can see for Succi Solace. This is
also the only song in the game that breaks the mold of how the rest of the games songs are themed or chosen, all else being long dead legends of the piano ect.

This is a dark gothic, 2D side-scroller, platformer-type game, made in PyGame with Python, that prioritizes heavy attention and effort put into creating a 
side-scroller that attempts meet the raised bar in regard to graphics and resolution. I have only been learning all of this for close to a year, so there are 
a lot of things that I end up re-creating and implementing back into the game, because I end up actually learning how to do it properly and have to go back and 
bring the level of graphics, style, structure, or accesories and features of the earlier creations up to be on par with the level of the later ones. This will 
hopefully continue to improve the game, as I learn new aspects of the creation process that I can practice and experiment with to give Succi Solace her own look 
and feel, to one day stand out in the indie game field amongst her peers.

There will be 12 or 13 levels in total.
